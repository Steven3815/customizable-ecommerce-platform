\# 1\. Project Overview

This project is a full-stack e-commerce platform designed for small businesses,  
allowing merchants to build their own product showcase and online shopping website.

The platform supports two modes:

\- Shopping Mode: Provides a complete shopping process including product browsing, shopping cart, orders, and payment.  
\- Showcase Mode: Allows merchants to create a product showcase website without enabling shopping and order functions.

The system mainly provides:

\- Product display and management  
\- Member management  
\- Shopping cart and order management  
\- Payment and refund management  
\- Delivery management  
\- Customer service system  
\- Store website customization
